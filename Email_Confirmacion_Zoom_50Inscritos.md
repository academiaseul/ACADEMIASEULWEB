## Paso 2 · Configurá tu cuenta (3 min)

1. **Profile** → subí foto tuya (la del Saturday shoot)
2. **Display Name:** `Jay Kim · Academia Seúl`
3. **Personal Meeting ID:** dejá el default
4. **Settings → Meeting → Cloud Recording:** ON
5. **Settings → Meeting → Waiting Room:** ON
6. **Settings → Meeting → Allow participants to rename themselves:** ON

1. **Meetings → Schedule a Meeting**
2. Completá EXACTO así:

Registration: Off
Security:
  ☑ Passcode (Zoom genera uno, copialo)
  ☑ Waiting Room
  ☐ Require authentication

Video:
  Host: On
  Participant: On

Audio: Both

Meeting Options:
  ☑ Allow participants to join anytime
  ☑ Mute participants upon entry
  ☐ Use Personal Meeting ID
  ☑ Automatically record meeting (Cloud)
  ☐ Approve or block entry to users from specific countries/regions
  ☑ Enable Q&A

3. **Save**
4. Copiás:
   - El **link** (ej: `https://us05web.zoom.us/j/12345678901?pwd=...`)

1. Click en el link de tu propio meeting (vos sos el host)
2. Verificá que la cámara y micrófono funcionan
3. Probá compartir pantalla
4. Cerrás el meeting (no lo termines, solo cerrá la ventana)
